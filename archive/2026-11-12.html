AI Ideas of the Day

AI side income

IndianAppGuy: a solo founder's 30 small products, built mostly by AI, passed $1 million in revenue

The founder runs a portfolio of small single-purpose tools such as MagicSlides, AskVideo and Sheet AI, mostly Chrome extensions and Google Workspace add-ons, entirely alone. He says AI writes most of the code (stack: Next.js, Supabase, Cloudflare). Per the interview, 8 of 30-plus products earn money, with about 1,000 paying customers a month and over $1 million in total revenue across roughly six years; the first two years brought none.

Multi-agent systems

AWS's official local emulator, written in Rust by a large group of AI agents

Per Simon Willison, aws-emulator lets developers run tests against local versions of many AWS services without a paid API key. It was written in Rust by a large group of agents, with 29,684 commits in the project's first three weeks, and AWS's Swami Sivasubramanian explained the build in a guest post on Werner Vogels' blog. It still needs Docker on macOS and Windows.
