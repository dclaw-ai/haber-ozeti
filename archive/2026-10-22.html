An AI operator audits freight invoices for a brokerage, 3,500 decisions in two months

MyFreightWorld uses an AI operator from Evos, trained on how experienced staff decide, to audit less-than-truckload carrier invoices against its transportation management system. It made nearly 3,500 audit decisions in two months with a claimed 97% accuracy (method not explained) and flagged over $600,000 in billing discrepancies in five weeks, which is not money recovered.
