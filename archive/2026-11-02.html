AI Ideas of the Day

Second brain

Obsidian vault run by Claude Code on a home server and reachable from a phone

A developer keeps an Obsidian vault written by Claude Code on a home server, where Obsidian Sync and Claude Code Remote Control run as systemd services. Rule, profile and map notes tell Claude where things go and what it may not touch (no deleting his text, no shell access), and nightly backups keep 14 copies. He captures ideas from his phone and asks questions such as "What did I decide about X?" to get answers linked to his own notes.
