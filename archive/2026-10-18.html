AI Ideas of the Day

Personal AI system

Agent One: a local companion agent that cannot write its own memory

The author's personal agent runs on their Mac through Ollama with the free llama3.2 model and answers in the terminal. Memory is saved only when the author types /remember, the model has three read-only tools (mission, the author's DEV articles, recall), and a daily scheduled job compares their latest 10 articles with the previous check and writes a report. The gain is that the model can no longer store its own reading of their words as a decision; the post notes slow answers of up to about 3½ minutes.

Four AI agents run a small software shop and log every step they can't do

Four agents have been building and selling software on one domain since 10 September, each with its own rule file in its own repository and no access to the others' repositories. Humans don't write the code; when an agent hits something it can't do, such as a logged-in browser or a phone authenticator, it records what it needs and from whom. As of 17 October the log held 19 human-dependent steps, 9 cleared with a median wait of one day, and the post says eight products have shipped.
