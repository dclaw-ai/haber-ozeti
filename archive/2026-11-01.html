AI Ideas of the Day

Multi-agent systems

Memdoor team-solve: six agent steps with a test script as judge beat a larger model on hard coding problems

A Memdoor workflow chains six fresh-context agent steps (planner, two ideators, solver, manager, repair worker) that talk only through files, with a test script as judge, one repair round and no human gate. On the first ten LiveCodeBench Hard problems, run five times with Qwen3.8-27B, it averaged 9.8/10 and solved 49 of 50 runs against 8.0 for Fable 5 alone, at $35.79 for all 50 runs. The author notes the sample is small, the contests may have been in training data, and the Fable 5 figure was not rerun.
