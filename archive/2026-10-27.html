AI side income

Vaultaire: an encrypted-vault iPhone app built and marketed almost entirely by coding agents

Founder Rasty Turek says Codex and Claude built the app in about three weeks without him writing code, and agents write all website copy. A custom tool connects agents to Google Search data, and each morning the agent finds keywords with impressions but few clicks and publishes about one targeted guide a day. He reports $41,000 in its best month and roughly $2,000–3,000 per day now, with over 60,000 downloads.
