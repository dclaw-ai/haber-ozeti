AI in operations

Flexport's CEO credits AI for 40% more volume on 5% more staff

Flexport says freight volumes are up 40% year on year while headcount is up 5%, a gap CEO Ryan Petersen attributes to AI. A new customs tool suggests 10-digit tariff codes with the supporting CBP rulings, and a licensed broker reviews each suggestion before filing; AI-written code for customer-specific delivery rules is likewise signed off by an expert. The article does not say how much of the growth AI causes, and the error-rate comparison with rivals comes from Flexport's own audits.

Multi-agent systems

18 AI subagents started at once and wrecked the repo

A developer had Claude create 18 subagents to pull tickets from a GitHub Project board for a multi-tenant SaaS, plus a QA agent to verify them, but all 18 started simultaneously from the same snapshot and overwrote each other's changes. Cards still moved to Ready for QA while tests failed, and untangling the branches took most of two days. The fix was to run one ticket at a time, which the author says proved faster in practice.
