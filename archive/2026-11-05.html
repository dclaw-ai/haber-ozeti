Canary: a song-based language app a non-developer vibe-coded and now earns about $25K a month

A founder with no coding background built Canary, an iOS app that teaches languages through songs, using AI coding tools; the first version took about a month and OpenAI models power features inside the app. He reports roughly $25,000 in revenue last month, 50,000 downloads and 2,000 paying subscribers about nine months after launch.

AI in operations

Double Nickel's AI agent chases driver employment checks and finishes them in one day instead of four

Double Nickel's agent Vera identifies a driver's former employers, sends verification requests and follows up by phone and email, with calls recorded and transcribed. In a 30-day beta at two carriers with 500+ trucks combined, it attempted about 250 checks and completed over 100 on its own (40%), averaging one day versus four for manual checks.
