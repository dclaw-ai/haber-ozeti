AI Ideas of the Day

AI in operations

Spartan Carrier Group lets dispatchers' hourly customer reports run on an AI agent

Texas carrier Spartan Carrier Group (about 150 trucks) built an agent on Alvys Foundry, inside its transport management system, that writes the hourly customer status reports dispatchers used to make by hand in Excel in 7 to 15 minutes. It went from setup to running in about a week and a half, matches dispatchers' output "almost 100%" and, by its VP of Operations' estimate, frees 17% of a dispatcher's day. A second agent for driver payroll is still untested.

AI side income

Voicy: a dictation app built with ChatGPT and Cursor that reports about $18K a month

Founder Kourosh built the first version of Voicy, a Whisper-based speech-to-text app for any computer or phone, in about two weeks by prompting ChatGPT and using Cursor, and says the Android app was built with Claude Opus. He reports about $11K in recurring revenue and $16–20K in total monthly revenue including lifetime deals, with roughly 1,400 subscriptions and 10,000 monthly users. Growth comes mainly from SEO (about 200 blog posts) and a YouTube series for seniors; figures are self-reported.

Multi-agent systems

LangChain's Content Studio: a Slack design agent with parallel subagents that works overnight

LangChain's in-house Content Studio uses one main "chief of staff" agent that splits a design request into tasks and hands them to asynchronous subagents, which produce videos, social graphics and slide decks as code. In one overnight job the main agent ran about an hour while subagents worked for more than six hours; anything published publicly needs human sign-off. LangChain reports over 3,000 assets and a median request cost that fell from about $8 to about 50 cents.
