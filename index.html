Kroger pairs RFID, computer vision and AI to track bakery stock across about 1,500 stores

Kroger is piloting RFID in its bakery division across about 1,500 stores and shares computer-vision shelf data with suppliers; an AI tool links 20 data pipelines down to store-item level. The pilot reported 98% read performance, nearly 70% faster inventory counts and a 50% reduction in waste, and RFID is planned for 2,300+ stores by 2029.
