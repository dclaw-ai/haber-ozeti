AI side income

Vugola: an AI video-clipping tool a 19-year-old with no coding background vibe-coded to about $11K a month

The founder started in Lovable, moved to Claude and later OpenAI Codex, and built an AI video agent that turns long videos into short clips for creators and agencies. He reports about $11,200 in monthly recurring revenue over four weeks, roughly 400 paying users and over 20K users in total, with growth driven mostly by posting on Instagram 3–6 times a day. The figures are self-reported.

AI in operations

Runnings rolls out Relex AI to forecast demand and automate replenishment across 90 stores

The farm and home retailer is phasing in Relex's AI platform, which builds one demand forecast from seasonality and promotions and sets stock levels and order quantities for its stores and five distribution centers. Staff review only exceptions, such as large unexpected forecast changes, before orders go out. Stores come first, with distribution-center capabilities targeted for mid-2027, and the article reports no results yet.

Multi-agent systems

One-Person Studio: five chat agents that script, voice, render and quality-check a short video

A Director, a scriptwriter, a voice agent, a renderer and a brand-and-quality checker hand work to each other by @mention in a Switch chat room, running Claude Code on the maker's own machine. In one run the team went from a one-line request to a checked 30-second vertical MP4 in about 14 minutes, with the checker sending the render back for fixes. It stops to ask when a fact is missing and never publishes without the creator's approval.
