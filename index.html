AI Ideas of the Day

Multi-agent systems

Eleven thousand bird stickers drawn by a fleet of Claude and Codex agents, with reviewer agents as quality control

One author used Claude Code as orchestrator, Codex with GPT-image to draw, and Claude subagents to find reference photos and score each sticker out of 100. At peak, 8 drawing sessions, 8 reviewers and 10 reference builders ran at once; failed stickers were redrawn with the reviewer's notes, up to twice. Result: 7,676 of 11,064 species met the 80-point bar, and the author reports that the reviewer needed recalibrating after a cross-check.
