AI in operations

Warrior Logistics runs Waabi's autonomous trucks on Dallas–Houston freight

Warrior, a carrier with more than 200 of its own trucks, uses Waabi Driver-powered autonomous trucks on routine food, beverage and consumer-goods loads between Dallas and Houston to add capacity alongside its drivers. Waabi says completed routes had 100% on-time pickup and delivery with zero safety incidents, and its Dallas–Houston load volume grew over 80% this year; neither company disclosed truck or load counts, and the trucks are not yet driverless.
