Best AI project of the week

Multi-agent systems

2,000 agents port a coding agent from TypeScript to Rust

Per a write-up of Prime Intellect's rewrite of its Prime Agent, over 2,000 agents ran across 10,000+ sandboxes for two weeks on a GLM-5.3 endpoint, with a root orchestrator and planner, implementer, reviewer and verifier roles per task. A pull request merged only after separate review and verification passed, with humans setting up the verification and reviewing results. Reported cold start fell from 736 ms to 52 ms and memory use from 1,130 MB to 237 MB, though the benchmarks are not independently replicated.
