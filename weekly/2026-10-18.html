Best AI project of the week

Every's company-wide Slack agent that turns winning workflows into shared skills

Every replaced its personal agents with one shared AI coworker in Slack that works in public threads and can save a successful workflow as a skill anyone on the team can run. It runs on Anthropic's Claude Managed Agents instead of self-hosted infrastructure. A model upgrade plus small fixes cut token costs across 11 common tasks by more than 80 percent.
